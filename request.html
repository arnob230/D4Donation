Request help from a charity

Tell us what you need. The charity will review your request and confirm whether they can help, and how.

The more specific you are, the easier it is for the charity to help quickly.


What happens next

  • The charity reviews your request and available supplies.
  • They confirm and arrange delivery, or decline with a reason.
  • The charity will contact you directly on the phone number you provide.
  • Requests are only visible to the charity you send them to.
