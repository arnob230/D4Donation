Donate to a charity

Tell us what you would like to give and where to collect it from. The charity will review your donation and confirm a pickup.



What happens next

  • The charity reviews your donation details.
  • They accept it and arrange a pickup, or decline with a reason.
  • The charity will contact you directly on the phone number you provide.
  • Once collected, the charity marks it as completed.
