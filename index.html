How D4Donation works

1

Pick a charity

Browse verified charities by cause: food, education, health, shelter or emergency relief.

2

Donate or request

List what you want to give (or what you need), add your name, phone and address, and send it to the charity.

3

Charity confirms

The charity reviews it, accepts or declines, then calls you to arrange pickup or delivery.

4

Done

The charity marks it as completed once the donation is collected or the help is delivered.
